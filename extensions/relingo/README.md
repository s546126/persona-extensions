# Relingo

A reading page with two modes: translation keeps the article and adds a translation under each block, and vocabulary underlines candidate words so they can be opened and remembered. The same saved words are available to the app through the `text_lookup` contribution: tap an English word anywhere in Persona to see its meaning and save it.

Relingo runs on its own. The dictionary is built into the program, and nothing needs to be installed or run on the command line.

This extension was `termwise` through version 0.1.2. Version 0.2.0 is `relingo` and declares that predecessor on the market entry, so an installed copy keeps its settings and data directory. Saved words stay in `reading.json`.

Relingo is also the name of an existing commercial English-vocabulary product. This extension is not that product and is not affiliated with it.
