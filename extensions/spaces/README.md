# Spaces

Spaces in Persona: manage any kind of thing with types, properties and relations. The structure is defined by the user; the page, boards, progress and the assistant's tools are all generated from it. Templates for projects and goals, reading and knowledge, inventory, household and chores, deals and coupons, and hosts and runbooks are included.
